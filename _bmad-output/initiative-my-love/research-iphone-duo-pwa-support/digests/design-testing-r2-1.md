### T4 — Tech Talk "Design for iPhone Duo"
- Outer (closed) display: top and bottom controls move to the RIGHT side, content to their left | https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111466/ | Apple | n.d. (© 2026) | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "Buttons and controls that normally sit at the top and bottom move to the right side of the display in order to maximize vertical space and to make them easier to reach with your right thumb. creating an uninterrupted area for your content to its left"
- The side column holds the tab bar, toolbars, back button, status bar and Dynamic Island | same URL | Apple | n.d. | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "This new space on the side houses the app controls. This includes the tab bar, the app toolbars, and navigation controls like the back button, the new redesigned status bar, and the Dynamic Island"
- Tab bar: stays bottom-aligned within the side column; top toolbar items go to its top, bottom ones to its bottom | same URL | Apple | n.d. | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "Toolbar buttons at the top of your app move to the top of this vertical space, while toolbar buttons on the bottom move to the bottom on iPhone Duo. And if you have a tab bar, it stays bottom aligned."
- Exception: items too wide for the side column (text buttons, segmented controls) stay in the nav bar; overflow collapses into a menu | same URL | Apple | n.d. | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "Toolbar items that are too wide for the space on the right, like a text button or a segmented control, should stay put in the nav bar on iPhone Duo." / "If there's not enough room, app controls will automatically collapse into an overflow menu."
- Inner display: controls also go to the side (edge not named); horizontal bars only in inner portrait | same URL | Apple | n.d. | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "Controls move to the side there as well, maximizing vertical space and keeping them within easy reach." / "The only pose where we've kept horizontal bars is on the inner display in portrait"
- Inner display option: present a tab bar as a sidebar (for information-dense apps) | same URL | Apple | n.d. | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "You can present your tab bar as a sidebar on the inner display. Now this isn't for every app."
- Split view on outer display: controls go to the outer edge — left edge for the left app | same URL | Apple | n.d. | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "When an app is on the left side, the controls move to the left edge of the display too"
- Partially folded: interactive elements move to the sides / bottom half; keep them off the hinge | same URL | Apple | n.d. | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "When the device is partially folded, interactive elements move to the bottom half of the display" / "Except for scrollable content, keep interactive elements away from the hinge as much as possible."
- Use safe-area insets / layout margins so UI is not obscured by side controls | same URL | Apple | n.d. | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "Build your interface using layout margins and safe area insets to avoid having your UI obscured."
- Sheets: controls move to the side on the outer display; inner display sheets use horizontal bars in both orientations | same URL | Apple | n.d. | accessed 2026-10-29 | high | design-guidance | "By default on the outer display, sheet controls also move to the side." / "On the inside, in both landscape and portrait, sheets use standard horizontal bars."
