## Not found

- **G1:** Nothing first-party newer than the 27.1 post (2026-10-23) changes the side-column behaviour, `viewport-fit=cover`, `env(safe-area-inset-*)` on iPhone Duo, the posture/segments flag status, or standalone web apps on iPhone Duo. Searched the full 27.2 post (2026-10-26, released) with the term list above: no matches. The only newer first-party items are bugs 328694 and 328695 (2026-10-23), which concern segment plumbing and testing and say nothing about flag status. UNVERIFIED whether the posture/segments flags changed in 27.2; the post says nothing either way.
- **G2:** No first-party source gives the values `env(safe-area-inset-top|right|bottom|left)` reports on iPhone Duo in any state, in Safari or in a Home Screen web app. None says what `viewport-fit=cover` does on iPhone Duo today. Searched the 27.2 post; Bugzilla quicksearch for "iPhone Duo", "safe-area-inset fold", "viewport-fit cover side" and "standalone web app safe area"; and three Tech Talk transcripts, which cover only native UIKit/SwiftUI safe areas. Bugs 325807 and 329103 cover iOS 27 Home Screen web apps on other iPhones (third-party reports) and are not Duo evidence.
- **G3:** No width for the side column in pt or CSS px. Talk 111462 says only that it is a "fixed width". Edge: the right edge in outer portrait is implied by "to the left of the status bar and camera" (talk 111461, native compatibility mode). Outer landscape and inner landscape have no stated edge; talk 111461 says vertical buttons "can appear on the left side in landscape", and talk 111462 says the inner-landscape position "remains consistent" with the outer display and is fixed to the hardware. Which physical edge that is in each landscape rotation is UNVERIFIED.
- **G4:** No first-party source states viewport sizes for a Home Screen (standalone) web app on iPhone Duo, sizes for the half-folded, book or laptop pose, segment rects, or devicePixelRatio. Searched the 27.2 post, three Tech Talk transcripts (grep for `points|pt|wide|[0-9]+ ?(pt|points)`: no numeric geometry) and Bugzilla. Nothing was derived from the Safari numbers.
- **G5:** No bugs.webkit.org bug was found that is specifically about iPhone Duo safe areas, side controls or standalone web apps on iPhone Duo. The quicksearches "iPhone Duo" (only unrelated hits), "safe-area-inset fold" (0 hits), "device posture" (0 hits), "viewport segments" (found 328694 and 328695 only) and the two viewport-fit/standalone queries turned up only general iOS 26/27 standalone bugs (e.g. 325807, 329103, 313800, 323403, 254868, 236445). Of those, only 325807 and 329103 were read.
- **G6:** Answered in part. The transcripts have no pose sizes and no side-column dimensions. They never mention web content, Safari or WKWebView; the only "Web"/"Safari & Web" hits are site navigation.
