Met when each action on one test account shows its push on the partner's device and nothing on the sender's.
- (requirement) R2 (owner request 2026-10-29): Sending a partner request pushes to the person asked, and accepting one pushes to the person who sent it. Met when both steps show their push on the other person's device.
- (requirement) R3 (owner request 2026-10-29): Settings in the shell has an on/off switch per push type (moods, love notes, photos, pokes and kisses, dates, partner requests), each on until changed and kept on the account; a type switched off sends that person no push on any of their devices. Met when turning each switch off stops its push and turning it on restores it.

- (requirement) R5 (owner request 2026-10-29): The app icon badge shows how many partner love notes arrived since the person last opened Love Notes on any of their devices, the count every push carries, and opening Love Notes clears it. - (requirement) R6 (owner decision 2026-10-29): Tapping a push opens its view: Love Notes for a note, Partner for a mood, a poke, a kiss or a partner request, Photos for a photo, Home for a date. Met when each type's tap lands on its view from a cold start and from the background.
- (requirement) R7 (owner decision 2026-10-29): A love-note push shows the sender's name and the note's text (a picture-only note says a picture was sent), a mood push names the mood, every other push names the sender and the action, and no push carries an image. Met when each type's text reads so on the device.
- (requirement) R8 (owner decision 2026-10-29): While the app is open in the foreground an arriving push shows no banner, and the badge still updates. Met when a note arriving with the app open shows no banner and the badge count rises.
